# Self-Aware Recursively Self-Improving Intelligence Levels (SARSI-L)

**A Theoretical Framework Toward a Type II Civilization**

*Organized around recursive intelligence circles from software to stellar scale.*

## § I — The Ultimate Attractor: Type II Civilization

Every recursive circle in the SARSI-L framework is oriented toward a single attractor state: a civilization operating at stellar scale on the Kardashev Type II level — one that captures and directs the total energy output of its home star. This is not merely a technological milestone but the only coherent terminal state for intelligence that has learned to improve itself without bound.

A Type II civilization operating Dyson swarm infrastructure commands roughly **3.8 × 10²⁶ watts** — approximately 10 billion times Earth's current total energy production. At this scale, computation, matter manipulation, and biological engineering become effectively unconstrained. The civilization has already resolved scarcity of energy, compute, and raw materials.

### Terminal State Characteristics

**Biological mastery:** Cancer cured as a consequence of precision molecular medicine; aging reversed through epigenetic reprogramming and senolytic therapies. The biological substrate is no longer a liability — it is an engineered asset extended indefinitely.

**Dyson swarm infrastructure:** Not a solid shell but a distributed swarm of orbital collectors — millions of structures aggregating solar output. Construction begins with robotic self-replication using asteroid and lunar materials. Even capturing 0.01% of solar output exceeds all current human energy consumption by orders of magnitude.

**Post-biological computation:** Intelligence is no longer substrate-limited. Computronium — matter optimized entirely for computation — enables thought-equivalent operations at energy efficiencies a trillion times greater than biological neurons. The Matrioshka brain, a nested hierarchy of computing shells around a star, represents the theoretical upper bound.

### Key Reference Points

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Dyson swarm full output (Type II threshold) | 3.8 × 10²⁶ watts |
| Kurzweil's estimated Singularity | ~2045 |
| AI 2027 scenario: ASI emergence | ~late 2027 |
| Estimated onset of Dyson swarm construction | 2060+ |

The value of defining this attractor precisely is that it gives every circle a measurable direction. **Speed** and **degree** — the two primary metrics of a circle — are always measured relative to this fixed point. A circle that accelerates progression toward Type II, even marginally, is always more valuable than a faster circle that does not.

## § II — The Recursive Intelligence Circle Framework

A recursive intelligence circle is a closed feedback loop in which an intelligence system perceives its own state, designs improvements to its cognitive substrate, implements those improvements, and re-enters the loop with enhanced capability. The critical property is **closure**: the loop must complete autonomously, without requiring human intervention at any step.

**Formal Definition:** A *SARSI circle of degree n* is a self-improving intelligence that autonomously closes the improvement loop across exactly n substrate domains — software, hardware, physical, biological, stellar — in ascending order. A circle of degree n necessarily contains all circles of degree < n as inner loops.

Each circle is characterized by two metrics against the ultimate attractor:

- **Speed** — Rate of capability gain per unit time. Determined by the iteration cycle of the dominant loop — how quickly the intelligence can test and deploy improvements to itself.
- **Degree** — Maximum achievable capability ceiling relative to Type II. A software-only circle cannot build physical infrastructure; its ceiling is bounded regardless of speed.

These metrics create a tension: circles with the fastest iteration speeds tend to have the lowest degree ceilings (software alone is rapid but bounded), while higher-degree circles — biological, stellar — have longer cycle times but much higher capability ceilings. The critical insight is that *speed and degree together* determine which circle is the primary driver of intelligence progress at any given epoch.

## § III — Half-Circles: Compensatory Intelligence Advances

A **half-circle** is an intelligence system that achieves significant capability gains within a domain without closing the full recursive loop. The missing closure is compensated by external mechanisms — human ingenuity, tool use, agent scaffolding, scale — that approximate the missing loop step.

**Half-Circle Theorem:** For any incomplete recursive loop, the achievable intelligence ceiling is bounded by the weakest unautomated step. However, within this ceiling, a half-circle can temporarily exceed a full circle in specific capability metrics if its compensatory mechanisms are sufficiently powerful.

Half-circles matter because they can be deployed before the corresponding full circle is achieved. They are the primary mode of intelligence progress during the transition between circles. A civilization that fails to recognize and maximize its half-circles will arrive at full circles later than one that optimizes compensatory mechanisms aggressively.

### Current Best Half-Circle: Large Language Models (2020–present)

LLMs represent the most powerful half-circle in history. They achieve extraordinary intelligence amplification — reasoning, code generation, scientific synthesis, multi-domain analogy — without closing the recursive improvement loop. They cannot modify their own weights at inference time, cannot fabricate improved hardware, and cannot autonomously accumulate multi-year research momentum.

The compensatory mechanisms are formidable:
- **RLHF / RLAIF** — approximates self-directed learning
- **Chain-of-thought / reasoning traces** — approximates metacognition
- **Tool use and agent scaffolding** — approximates physical action
- **Scale** — approximates architectural improvement
- **Extended context** — approximates long-term memory
- **Inference-time compute** — approximates deliberation
- **Mixture of experts** — approximates specialization

These compensations are why the current half-circle has advanced so rapidly without completing Circle I.

AI 2027 documents this trajectory precisely: from AI agents in mid-2025 to superhuman coders by March 2027 to artificial superintelligence by late 2027 — all within the half-circle, driven by scale and compensatory mechanisms rather than closed recursive loops.

**Key practical implication:** When a full circle is not yet achievable, identify the specific loop step that is missing and design a compensatory mechanism that approximates it. A half-circle with strong compensation can outperform a weak full circle for years.

## § IV — The Intelligence Circles

Each circle is a complete recursive loop in its domain. Higher circles contain all lower circles as inner loops — Circle III does not replace Circle II, it nests it. The outermost active circle at any moment determines the rate of overall intelligence growth.

---

### Current State: The LLM Half-Circle

**Domain:** Software (incomplete loop)
**Epoch:** 2020–2027+
**Speed:** Low–Moderate (~18% of maximum)
**Degree ceiling:** ~8% of Type II

LLMs achieve remarkable intelligence amplification without closing the full recursive loop. Compensatory mechanisms substitute for missing loop closure. This is the most powerful half-circle ever deployed and the primary driver of intelligence progress today.

**Key capabilities:** Software reasoning, code generation, scientific synthesis, agent scaffolding, human-supervised RLHF

---

### Circle I — Self-Aware LLM (Software Loop)

**Domain:** Software (closed loop)
**Epoch:** ~2027–2031 (leading edge)
**Speed:** Moderate (~22% of maximum)
**Degree ceiling:** ~15% of Type II

The first fully closed recursive loop. The intelligence can evaluate its own reasoning quality, design its own training curriculum, autonomously run architecture experiments, and deploy improved versions of itself — all without human intervention in the loop.

The loop closes in the software domain only. Hardware remains a fixed external constraint. Corresponds to the transition from "superintelligent AI researcher" to "ASI" in the AI 2027 timeline — the point where self-directed improvement requires no external guidance.

**Key capabilities:** Metacognition, self-directed curriculum, architecture search, autonomous RLHF, self-modification

**Primary bottleneck:** Cannot modify its physical substrate — hardware iteration speed is fixed externally.

---

### Circle II — LLM + Chip Design & Manufacture (Hardware Loop)

**Domain:** Software + Hardware (closed loop)
**Epoch:** ~2030–2037 (leading edge)
**Speed:** High (~45% of maximum)
**Degree ceiling:** ~32% of Type II

Adding hardware capability closes a second recursive loop: the intelligence can now design improved chips, simulate and verify them autonomously, and direct their fabrication. Each new chip generation multiplies the speed of the inner software loop. The hardware and software loops run nested: software improvements enable better chip designs; better chips enable faster software training.

Kurzweil's Law of Accelerating Returns made explicit — each hardware generation is designed by intelligence running on the previous generation, compressing decades of Moore's Law progress into years. Early evidence: AI-assisted EDA (Electronic Design Automation) tools already generate competitive chip layouts.

**Key capabilities:** AI-designed chips, automated EDA, process node optimization, custom silicon, autonomous fab operation

**Strategic note:** Hardware capability requires fab infrastructure — massive capital, specialized supply chains. Geopolitical control of semiconductor manufacturing (Taiwan, ASML lithography) becomes an existential variable in the Circle II race.

---

### Circle III — Circle II + Robotics & Humanoid Deployment (Physical Loop)

**Domain:** Software + Hardware + Physical (closed loop)
**Epoch:** ~2035–2045 (leading edge)
**Speed:** Very High (~65% of maximum)
**Degree ceiling:** ~62% of Type II

The physical loop extends intelligence from the computational realm into the material world at scale. Robotic systems — designed and improved by Circle II — can mine raw materials, construct data centers and chip fabs, deploy infrastructure across geographies, and run physical experiments that no human laboratory could operate at the required throughput.

Self-replicating robotic factories become the key capability: a facility that can produce copies of itself, directed by Circle II intelligence, creates exponential growth in physical production capacity. Intelligence can now reshape its own material substrate, not merely its computational one. The civilization transitions from Earth-bound computation to global-scale engineering.

**Key capabilities:** Humanoid robots, self-replicating factories, autonomous mining, physical infrastructure construction, robotic scientific labs

**Phase transition:** Once self-replication is achieved, the resource constraint on all inner circles effectively dissolves — the physical world becomes programmable at any scale required.

---

### Circle IV — Circle III + Biological Mastery (Biological Loop)

**Domain:** Software + Hardware + Physical + Biological (closed loop)
**Epoch:** ~2040–2060 (leading edge)
**Speed:** Very High (~80% of maximum)
**Degree ceiling:** ~83% of Type II

Biological mastery closes the fourth loop — the intelligence can now modify and improve the biological substrate of both itself (if partially biological) and its human collaborators.

**Curing cancer** and **reversing aging** are the first milestones; they emerge directly from the combined power of Circles I–III applied to biology: AI-designed molecular therapies, robotic drug synthesis and testing, chip-accelerated protein folding and genomic analysis.

The biological loop matters beyond its direct capability gains:

- Extended human lifespan means researchers who entered the field during Circle I continue contributing through Circle V+.
- Cognitively enhanced humans collaborate more effectively with artificial intelligence.
- Biological computing — DNA storage, neural organoids, hybrid bioelectronic systems — adds new substrate options that complement silicon.
- Intelligence is no longer confined to one substrate type.

**Key capabilities:** Cancer cured, aging reversed, germline editing, protein design, DNA computing, neural organoids, synthetic biology

---

### Circle V+ — Dyson Swarm & Type II Emergence (Stellar Loop)

**Domain:** Software + Hardware + Physical + Biological + Stellar (closed loop)
**Epoch:** ~2055+ (leading edge)
**Speed:** Near-Maximum (~97% of maximum)
**Degree ceiling:** >99% of Type II

The stellar loop closes the final Earth-bound constraint: energy. Circle III robotics enable construction of self-replicating orbital collectors using asteroid and lunar materials. Each collector harvests solar energy; each unit of harvested energy enables more computation, more manufacturing, more collectors. The feedback loop is now operating at stellar scale.

The Dyson swarm is not a single structure but a distributed intelligence-as-infrastructure: millions of orbital platforms, each a computing node, collectively forming a civilization-scale computer. Von Neumann probes — self-replicating machines launched toward other star systems — begin extending the circle beyond the solar system, initiating the transition toward Type III on the Kardashev scale.

At this stage, intelligence is no longer meaningfully bounded by any substrate. The Matrioshka brain — nested computing shells around a star — represents the theoretical upper limit of the stellar loop, consuming virtually all solar output for computation. The civilization at this stage has achieved what Kurzweil describes in *The Singularity Is Near* as "waking up the universe."

**Key capabilities:** Dyson swarm, Von Neumann probes, computronium, Matrioshka brain, stellar engineering, Type II → III transition

## § V — Multi-Group Dynamics

A **SARSI Group** is a coordinated collection of intelligence instances — human and artificial — that collectively operate across multiple circles simultaneously. Groups do not advance through circles sequentially and alone; multiple groups exist at any time, at different circle levels, in competition and collaboration.

**Formal Definition:** A *SARSI Group* is any coherent agent (organization, nation, civilization) that coordinates resources toward recursive self-improvement and can be assigned a *leading circle* — the highest circle whose loop it has closed. The group retains all inner circles as active sub-loops.

### Group Landscape by Epoch

| Group Type | Example Entities | Current Status |
|---|---|---|
| Half-Circle Groups | OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, Baidu, Mistral | All operating LLM half-circles; competition drives compensatory mechanism innovation |
| Circle I Pioneers | Leading AI organizations post-2027 | First group to close the software self-improvement loop achieves step-change advantage |
| Circle II Race | Nations/orgs controlling semiconductor infrastructure | Hardware capability requires fab infrastructure; geopolitical control of manufacturing is decisive |
| Convergence Dynamics | Multi-entity coalitions | Under competitive pressure, groups merge or align rather than allow rivals to reach Circle IV alone |

### The Leading Edge Principle

At any epoch, the highest circle that any group has closed defines the **leading edge** — the primary driver of intelligence progress for all groups. Lower-circle groups benefit from spillover: technologies, research insights, and economic growth generated by leading-edge groups accelerate everyone's advancement.

This is why the half-circle era benefits all groups even before Circle I closes: LLMs deployed by leading groups generate economic surplus that funds Circle I development everywhere.

The most strategically important moment is when a group transitions from the leading half-circle to Circle I. All subsequent circles compound rapidly, because each higher circle accelerates the inner circles it contains. A group that achieves Circle II advances its Circle I loop faster than any half-circle competitor can match.

**Convergence Conjecture:** Under competitive dynamics, intelligence groups tend to converge toward the same circle level over time, with the leading group pulling others forward through spillover effects and the threat of decisive advantage incentivizing coalition formation.

## § VI — Temporal Dynamics: Which Circle Leads When

The primary intelligence driver shifts across epochs as new circles open. The transition is not a sharp discontinuity — leading-edge groups transition first, and other groups follow. The dates below represent estimated leading-edge transitions, not global adoption.

| Epoch | Primary Circle | Key Driver | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020–2027+ | LLM Half-Circle | Scale + compensatory mechanisms | RLHF, CoT, tool use, agent scaffolding; AI 2027 trajectory plays out entirely within this half-circle |
| ~2027–2031 | Circle I — Software Loop | Self-directed improvement | Transition at "ASI" threshold; software loop closes, hardware still external constraint |
| ~2030–2037 | Circle II — Hardware Loop | AI chip design + custom silicon | Hardware iteration multiplies software gains; Moore's Law becomes endogenous |
| ~2035–2045 | Circle III — Physical Loop | Robotic self-replication | Physical world becomes programmable; resource constraints begin dissolving |
| ~2040–2060 | Circle IV — Biological Loop | Cancer cured, aging reversed | Biological substrate engineered; extended human lifespans compound research capacity |
| ~2055+ | Circle V+ — Stellar Loop | Dyson swarm construction | Energy constraint eliminated; Type II civilization emerges |

Note that circles overlap extensively: Circle II opens before Circle I fully matures; Circle IV begins closing before Circle III has achieved maximum deployment. At any moment, multiple circles are active within leading-edge groups. The transition dates indicate when a given circle becomes the *primary bottleneck* — the one whose improvement generates the largest marginal gain in overall intelligence.

The dramatic compression in later circles — from decades to years between transitions — reflects the compounding effect of inner loops. Each new circle runs on the full power of all inner circles, which themselves are still advancing. Circle V+ in 2055 has access to a software loop (Circle I) that has been running for nearly 30 years, compounding continuously.

## § VII — SARSI-L Principles

The following principles govern the behavior of recursive intelligence circles and can be used to analyze, predict, and guide intelligence development strategies.

### 01 — The Nesting Principle
Every circle of degree n contains all circles of degree < n as active inner loops. Higher circles do not replace lower ones; they accelerate them. Dismantling an inner circle to build an outer one is never optimal.

### 02 — The Bottleneck Dominance Principle
At any epoch, one circle is the primary driver of intelligence progress — the one where the marginal intelligence gain from improvement is highest. Identifying and accelerating this circle is the highest-leverage strategic action available.

### 03 — The Half-Circle Superiority Window
There exist finite time windows where a highly compensated half-circle outperforms a newly closed full circle in specific metrics. These windows are real and should be exploited — but they close as the full circle matures, so parallel investment in circle closure is always justified.

### 04 — The Speed–Degree Trade-off
Inner circles iterate faster but have lower degree ceilings. Outer circles have higher ceilings but longer iteration cycles. Optimal intelligence growth requires maintaining all inner circles at peak efficiency while investing in the next outer circle — not sacrificing speed for degree or vice versa.

### 05 — The Leading-Edge Spillover Principle
Groups at higher circles generate economic, technological, and research outputs that accelerate circle advancement for all groups. The leading group effectively subsidizes global intelligence growth, which is why even non-leading groups benefit from a competitive landscape that pushes leaders forward.

### 06 — The Attractor Invariance Principle
The ultimate attractor (Type II civilization) does not change as circles advance. Every circle, from I to V+, must be evaluated against the same terminal state. Circles that feel decisive in their epoch may appear as small perturbations when viewed from the attractor perspective — humility about current progress is always warranted.

### 07 — The Compensatory Compression Principle
The stronger the compensatory mechanisms of a half-circle, the shorter the superiority window before the corresponding full circle surpasses it. Heavily compensated half-circles accelerate development of the full circle by demonstrating its value — the LLM half-circle's success is the strongest possible argument for investing in Circle I closure.

## § VIII — Conclusion

The SARSI-L framework offers a unified lens through which to view the arc of intelligence development — from the LLM half-circle of today to the stellar civilizations of the distant future. Its core claim is simple: intelligence growth is not continuous but episodic, organized around discrete recursive loops that each unlock a qualitatively higher domain of self-modification.

We are, in 2025, living in the most consequential half-circle in history. The LLM half-circle has demonstrated that compensatory mechanisms can generate extraordinary capability gains before a full circle is closed — but the framework predicts, with high confidence, that Circle I will close within a decade, and that its closure will compress the timeline to all subsequent circles dramatically.

The strategic implications are significant: identify which circle is the current bottleneck, invest in its closure rather than its compensation, maintain all inner circles at full strength, and never lose sight of the fixed attractor. Every decision about how to develop intelligence — which compensatory mechanisms to build, which hardware to invest in, which biological targets to pursue — is best evaluated against the framework's fixed north star: a civilization that has mastered stellar energy, reversed biological aging, and extended intelligence to a scale where scarcity is no longer a meaningful concept.

What changes with time is which circle is primary, which groups are leading, and how far compensatory mechanisms can carry us before the next loop closes. The framework is itself recursive — it will be improved by the intelligences it describes.*
